The framework

A system, not a silver bullet

Three stages, each building on the last. Work through them in sequence — don't skip to corrections before you've mapped the pattern.

Core principle

Control vs Resolution

❌ Control (temporary)

Constant effort to suppress emotions. Like dumping buckets from a leaky ceiling — it never fixes the leak. Mental energy drained daily. Upside capped by training wheels.

✓ Resolution (permanent)

Correct the underlying flaw and the emotion stops triggering entirely. No management needed. Free to focus completely on execution — the emotion is simply gone.

The signal model

Emotion as data

Just as you read chart signals to find trade setups, emotions are signals of hidden performance flaws.

⚡
Trigger
A loss, a miss, a stop-out. Instant reflex — no conscious thought involved.
📡
Signal
The emotion that fires. Don't fight it — read it. It's pointing to a hidden flaw.
🔍
Root flaw
The real cause — a belief, bias, or illusion running at the unconscious level.
